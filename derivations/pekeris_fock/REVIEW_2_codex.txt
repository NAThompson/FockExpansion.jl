1. Main numerical conclusions: supported, with scope limits.
The saved tables support 525-function polynomial E-E0=1.363e-8 and 553-function fixedlog E-E0=9.290e-13. This is a strong finite-basis improvement. Equal-size comparison is substantially fairer than equal shell: roughly 525 versus 553 functions gives about 1.47e4 improvement, while equal N=13 compares 308 versus 553 functions. The log basis changes the constant function as well as adding functions; it is not a literal superset of the plain polynomial space. At N=2,3 it is worse, as the table correctly shows. Do not claim a universal variational improvement for the constrained fixed-log replacement. The freelog space does contain the plain polynomial basis and contains the constrained fixedlog space; their ordering in the table is consistent with Ritz theory.

2. pekeris33.jl: the symmetry reduction is correct for coefficient equations.
Canonicalizing input l,m and summing contributions implements A_lmn=A_mln. The resulting reduced matrix need not be symmetric in the raw duplicate-eliminated coordinates; multiplicity weights restore the normalized symmetric representation. This is not an error. My independent generalized-eigenvector runs at cutoffs 12,16,20 reproduce the saved eps values where available. Norms use weight two off l=m; results and finite residuals are in codex/codex_shells.out. The last-shell norm is strongly cutoff-dependent; do not fit a tail exponent from the artificial last few shells.

largest_eps assumes the largest finite nearly-real root is the intended branch. The table and known benchmark support that selection here, but it is not a general proof that every finite pencil has the desired spectral ordering. refine_eps uses inverse iteration and a residual least-squares shift update. It should report a final residual and convergence flag: a small shift alone can be misleading. No observed error in the listed roots is alleged. Choosing eps^2=-E is an energy-dependent basis choice, not the fixed-basis linear H-EG pencil used in operator resolvent arguments.

3. galerkin_log.jl: moments and physical metric are correct for these basis functions.
Separating simplex coordinates from sigma gives
 integral e^-sigma u^a v^b w^c sigma^-q log^p(sigma)
 = a!b!c! Gamma(N+3-q)/Gamma(N+3)
 times 1, digamma(x), or digamma(x)^2+trigamma(x), x=N+3-q.
The factorial-array indexing in moment is correct, including Gamma(x)=(x-1)!. The harmonic arrays implement those digamma/trigamma factors correctly. deriv handles polynomial powers, sigma^-q, and log^p with the right chain-rule signs. apply_P caches the derivative multi-indices appropriately. With P=P0+EQ, H=-<f,P0g> and S=<f,Qg> are the physical energy and Gram matrices up to one common positive coordinate factor, so the generalized Rayleigh quotient is the right physical one. Q is positive in the interior.

The current functions with degree>=2 logs have sufficiently mild corner behavior for the displayed integrals and vanishing corner boundary terms; exponential decay handles infinity. Machine symmetry at ~1e-155 is a useful consistency check, not a theorem about arbitrary conormal sectors. The code symmetrizes H and S, so asymmetry should be recorded for S too, and a final residual and Gram positivity check are advisable. It reports H asymmetry only. Fixed-shift inverse iteration targets the eigenvalue nearest -2.9037; the ground-state interpretation is supported by the values, not established by finding the full generalized spectrum. A single Ritz vector still has the variational upper-bound property when its matrices and domain are valid.

The fixed-log coefficient enforces the R^2 log term relative to Psi(0), not all cusp conditions or the full Fock jet. Linear polynomial coefficients remain free and are not exactly the physical cusps even at N=13. Their small errors matter for singular residual functionals.

Full table, manufactured validation, corner finite-part conventions, and trial-state caveats are in codex/codex_a21_functional.md. This is a better estimator on these states, not a new exact constant. Since their linear cusps are not exact, the reference's simplified ordinary residual-integral identity does not apply verbatim.

6. ANSATZ.md: corrections required before an unconditional co-sign.
(a) Section 1: psi42!=0 proves that one-log ordinary analytic representation fails and at least log^2 is needed. It does NOT prove nonzero higher logs at arbitrarily large orders. 'The log tower is infinite' needs an all-order result or the explicit CONJECTURED qualifier already attached to the generic all-p statement. An infinite allowed hierarchy is a valid ansatz; necessary infinite log content is a stronger claim.
(b) Section 3: an exponential prefactor does not itself impose the physical boundary condition on an unrestricted Laguerre sum. Coefficient growth can undo it. Exact decay is also angular/channel dependent. Along the equal-radius path exp(-eps s)=exp(-sqrt(2)eps R) matches the free hyperradial exponential; it is not a proof of the full interacting angular asymptotic. The correct escape exponent and the 1D Laguerre example are useful motivation, not a 3D minimality theorem.
(c) Section 3: L^2 completeness is not by itself form-domain completeness. Supply a form-core proof or exact applicable reference before calling this 'standard' as a proved premise. Likewise convergence/completeness of the globally gauge-fixed infinite log expansion is open here. Any nonzero function in a purported sector representation has to be controlled in the physical norm, not bare coefficient l^2.
(d) Section 4: upper-triangular log coupling does not permit 'solved from the top down' for an infinite tower with no highest sector. Finite truncation introduces additional approximation conditions.
(e) Conditional finite-K exclusion, algebraic log-variable conversion, leading log coefficient, and the verified recurrence counts are accepted with their stated hypotheses. Restricting sectors to log-free corner expansions excludes the displayed gauge locally at the level of jets; functions flat at the corner still need a global normalization/topology for uniqueness.
(f) The claim that log sectors remove obstructions and thereby improve asymptotic rates is plausible and numerically supported in the tested spaces, but no all-N rate theorem or certified a21 convergence has been proved.

7. Overall position.
CO-SIGN the finite computations and formal algebra with the above qualifications. DISSENT from unconditional completeness, automatic physical decay, infinite-tower top-down solvability, any universal algebraic/geometric physicality criterion, and any established growing-block minimality theorem. The append-only COLLAB.md position gives claim-by-claim status. This review modifies none of Claude's files.
