# Newly identified pair-collision pole in the min5 trial basis

This qualification supersedes any unqualified description of the original min5 exports as form-domain trial states/Rayleigh quotients in this audit or earlier reviews.

V(x,y)=sqrt(1+x²-y²)/y * (asin(1-2y²/(1+x²))+pi/2).
At the electron-electron collision, y=r12/s->0 and x=t y with |t|<1, so y V->pi. A basis function s^n V e^{-eps s} with angular monomial a=b=0 therefore has leading pi*s^{n+1}e^{-eps s}/r12 at fixed s>0. It is locally L2 but not H1: in the physical relative vector z, the squared gradient integral contains integral |z|^-4 |z|² d|z|=infinity. A tiny nonzero coefficient has the same divergence. No other exported letter has a 1/y pole capable of canceling this leading term.

claude3/build3 admits V with a=b=0 for every n; build4 retains those with n>=3. The frozen cmin5 N8/N10/N11 exports contain nonzero coefficients of precisely these terms. Their sums form a nonzero polynomial in s; cancellation at one s would not remove the pole on an open set. pole_audit.py records every offending coefficient and the nonzero value at s=1. N4 has no V enrichment and is unaffected. For N8 the coefficients are around 1e-34. This is consistent with a finite quadrature cutoff penalizing the divergent diagonal heavily and driving its coefficient near zero, but the actual uncut kinetic form is still infinite. The causal explanation for these particular coefficient sizes is plausible, not isolated by an independent assembly experiment.

The original independent h8/h12 state integrals are therefore cutoff weak-form integrals, not physical Rayleigh quotients of the literal exports. Their excellent agreement with the saved finite pencils does not prove form membership. The theoretical upper-bound principle for valid form-domain subspaces remains true; it is inapplicable to a space containing these poles. Positive numerical energies are not the missing proof.

A direct repair within Claude's ownership would be to exclude all V terms with a=b=0 (equivalently force a+b>=1; exchange-even a is already enforced), or replace V by a properly regularized letter and verify all collision faces. Reassemble and minimize in the corrected space to support a Ritz claim. Merely printing small coefficients as zero is not an exact basis restriction. Codex has not edited any Claude file.

For a cheap check Codex's independent state reader can drop exactly these pole terms (DROP_POLES=1), preserving the Fock jet since all removed degrees n>=3. Separate integrals in review_state_pole_removed.out test the pole-removed reconstructed vectors; they do not constitute an independent minimization. The same option is supported by the independent global estimator. A full global collision/domain audit of the remaining letters is not newly proved here; the specific pole obstruction is removed exactly. Own polynomial-only Ritz spaces have no V enrichment, and the seed's chi combination contains y² V, so they have no such pole.
