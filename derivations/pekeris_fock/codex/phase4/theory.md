# Constrained readout: what convergence would require

The seed is e^{-eps s}[1+(P1+eps s)+(P2+eps s P1+eps²s²/2)+B2 log R], where P1=r12/2-Zs, P2=R² psi20(a21=0), B2=-Z kappa D. Every bracketed expression is grouped by its homogeneous degree. Thus its physical degree <=2 expansion is 1+P1+P2+B2 log R. Adding a D e^{-eps s} changes only the degree-two harmonic coefficient at this order. Every enrichment here has degree >=3 and hence contributes no such jet. For c0 !=0 the ratio a/c0 is exactly the trial function's normalized local a21, including the total-projection convention inherited from psi20. This algebra does not claim it is the exact eigenfunction's a21.

Form density by itself does NOT prove consistency of this readout. In six physical dimensions choose a smooth radial cutoff eta, eta=1 near zero, compactly supported, and let w_h=c D eta(R/h). This is singlet, has zero constant/linear/log jets and degree-two harmonic coefficient c. Scaling gives ||w_h||_L2=O(|c| h^5) and ||grad w_h||_L2=O(|c| h^4); Coulomb form contributions are O(c² h^9). Thus w_h ->0 in form norm with fixed c, or even c=h^{-p}, 0<p<4, while its a21 jet stays nonzero or diverges. Adding this bump to the exact state preserves all specified jets except the free a21. After normalization its energy defect is O(c² h^8), using the eigenfunction weak equation. There is no universal bound |delta a21| <= C*(delta E)^alpha for any alpha>0 on this constrained class. The counterexample shows insufficiency of the stated form-density hypothesis; it does not prove divergence of the particular numerical Ritz sequence.

Point value c0 is also not continuous in six-dimensional H1. A separate lower bound/nonzero limit for c0 is needed for the ratio. Density of a constrained union can be compatible with arbitrary local coefficients because point jets have zero form capacity at these orders.

Conditional consistency is valid if normalized Ritz states converge in a topology controlling the full blown-up-corner expansion through degree 2 (including angular projection and a uniform remainder), with c0 converging to a nonzero value. Form convergence and elliptic interior estimates away from the triple corner do not supply that hypothesis. Uniform weighted corner regularity/residual estimates would be required. If a readout were bounded in a stronger error norm and that norm were O(sqrt(delta E)), the usual linear observable error would be O(sqrt(delta E)); no such bound follows here. Energy is stationary, the corner coefficient is not.

Residual correction: let f=P_v[Psi], b=-Z kappa and g=f''+5f'/R-12f/R²-[2P_v[V Psi]-2E f]. Then g=2P_v[(E-H)Psi]. The decaying radial Green formula gives
 a_local = -1/8 FP integral [2P_v[V Psi]-2E f+g]/R dR - b/8.
Consequently a_global=-1/8 FP integral [2P_v[V Psi]-2E f]/R dR-b/8
 = a_local + 1/4 FP integral P_v[(E-H)Psi]/R dR.
It is an exact residual-correction identity for the trial state's local coefficient when the finite parts and endpoint assumptions hold. The global estimator omits the residual contribution in the ODE reconstruction. This singular residual functional is also not bounded by energy error. It is cheap with exact radial moments and may improve a numerical estimate, but is no guaranteed improvement or rigorous enclosure.

For a bounded regularized observable J_delta (a fixed-radius angular/radial average), an adjoint solve can cancel its first-order state error; the normalization/eigenvalue and adjoint approximation terms must be included. Passing delta->0 requires uniform estimates and is not a free error-square theorem for a21.

In these constrained states the residual integral in the correction is ordinary: exact constant/linear/order-two Fock jets cancel the projected singular and constant residual terms, leaving P_v[(H-E)Psi]=O(R log^k R) near zero. The Eseed-Etrial mismatch contributes a constant angular function at order zero, annihilated by P_v; its first nonzero projected term is order R. Thus
 a_direct-a_global = (1/4) integral_0^infinity P_v[(H-Etrial)Psi]/R dR.
The potential/energy integral defining a_global still requires the finite part. The distinction is essential. The manufactured radial f=R²e^{-R}, h=0 check gives integral (Lf)/R=-8, verifying the residual sign in verify_jet.py.
