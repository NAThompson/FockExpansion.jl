# x20 primary references and scope

Accessed 2026-10-23 using read-only browsing. Mathematical calculations and
specialized certificates are original to x20; references supply stated general
theorems, not the numerical Picard numbers of these surfaces.

1. M. Kuwata and T. Shioda, *Elliptic parameters and defining equations for
   elliptic fibrations on a Kummer surface*, arXiv:math/0609473, §2.1, Table 1.
   https://arxiv.org/html/math/0609473#S2.SS1
   Full text read. Explicit J1 equation, its construction from two Legendre
   curves and its generic MW group are the external identification used.
   We independently match all coefficients after the 2-isogeny.

2. R. van Luijk, *K3 surfaces with Picard number one and infinitely many
   rational points*, §2; especially p.6 (PDF page index 5).
   https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~luijkrmvan/ps/picone.pdf
   Full relevant text read. Specialization upper bounds, discriminant
   squareclass comparison, ordinary K3 Tate/Artin–Tate input and square
   Brauer order for elliptic surfaces. The paper points to Nygaard–Ogus,
   Thm.0.2 and Milne, Thm.6.1. No unresolved general Tate conjecture is
   invoked for our ordinary characteristic >5 examples.

3. M. Schütt and T. Shioda, *Elliptic surfaces*, arXiv:0907.0298,
   Cor.6.13 (Shioda–Tate), Cor.7.5 (torsion/component injection), §7.7
   (torsion quotient).
   https://arxiv.org/html/0907.0298
   Relevant full text read. This research survey supplies the explicit
   general statements; the rank calculations and section characters here
   are independent applications, not attributed results for S_a.

4. Yu. G. Zarhin, *Hodge groups of K3 surfaces*, J. reine angew. Math.341
   (1983), 193–220, DOI 10.1515/crll.1983.341.193.
   https://doi.org/10.1515/crll.1983.341.193
   Original publication metadata checked; original full text was not
   accessible through the browsing endpoints used. The precise cited Hodge
   statements were inspected in Daniel Huybrechts's author-hosted
   *Lectures on K3 Surfaces*, Ch.3, Thms.3.7/3.9 and Remark3.14:
   https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/huybrech/K3Global.pdf
   Huybrechts gives an elementary proof of the field statement. The
   endomorphism-field and Hodge-group theorem are CITED inputs, not
   independently re-proved by a computer experiment. For rank five the
   exclusion of a degree-five totally-real field is explicitly given in x20.

5. Y. André, *Mumford–Tate groups of mixed Hodge structures and the theorem
   of the fixed part*, Compositio Math.82 (1992), 1–24, §5, Thm.1.
   https://www.numdam.org/article/CM_1992__82_1_1_0.pdf
   Relevant original full text read. Connected monodromy is normal in the
   derived generic Mumford–Tate group for the geometric polarized good
   variation. The fixed-part argument and nonisotriviality are stated
   separately in our application; the theorem does not infer a physical
   period pairing or give an explicit PF operator.

Context checked but NOT used as an identification of S_e2: N. Braeger,
A. Clingher, A. Malmendier, S. Spatig, *Isogenies of certain K3 surfaces
of rank 18*, arXiv:2109.03189, §3. It distinguishes a product-period
isogeny from an isomorphism to a Kummer. No resemblance to one of its
normal forms was promoted to a proof about our surfaces.
