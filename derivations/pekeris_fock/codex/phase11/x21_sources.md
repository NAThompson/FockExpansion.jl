1. P. Deligne, *Théorie de Hodge II*, Publ. Math. IHES 40 (1971), 5–57.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1971__40__5_0.pdf
   Relevant original text read: Thm. 2.3.5 (strictness/exact graded functor),
   §§3.1–3.2 (residues and the weight spectral sequence), Cor. 3.2.17
   (lowest weight is the compactification image). Our bare-K3 application
   distinguishes ambient occurrence from the physical framing.

2. P. Deligne, *Théorie de Hodge III*, Publ. Math. IHES 44 (1974), 5–77.
   https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1974__44__5_0.pdf
   Relevant original text read: Ex. 8.3.8 and Prop. 8.3.9, printed pp.43–44
   (PDF pp.40–41): relative cohomology MHS, exact relative sequence, and
   construction from the filtered logarithmic mapping cone. This does not
   calculate the unconstructed full psi40 compactification for us.

3. F. Brown, *Feynman amplitudes, coaction principle, and cosmic Galois
   group*, arXiv:1512.06409v3, §§0.2, 9.1.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.06409
   Relevant original text read: period as a specified de Rham/Betti matrix
   coefficient, and the smallest quotient detected by the Betti framing.
   This is methodological context only. No graph-specific theorem is
   asserted for the psi40 integral.
