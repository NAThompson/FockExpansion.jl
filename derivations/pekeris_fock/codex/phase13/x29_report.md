## Calibration function and exact operator

On -1<u<0 use the real analytic branch

    m=(u-1)/(u+1),
    p=K(m)/sqrt(-u(u+1)),
    g=(1+u)p+u p',
    t=2log(sqrt(1-u^2))+log(-u)
        +log(sqrt(1+u))^2+Li_2(-u),
    f=g+t.

Here K uses the elliptic parameter, not the modulus. The constant factor
relating p to x25's possibly complex branch has no differential effect.
This is a calibration of the requested elliptic-plus-Tate shape, not a fit
to the magnitude or singular coefficients of the physical B_A.

Let l=log(1+u). Then

    t'=a+b*l,
    a=-2u/(1-u^2)+1/u,
    b=1/(2(1+u))-1/u,
    l'=1/(1+u).

The script builds the rational connection in (p,p',1,l,t), generates exact
observed derivative rows, and obtains G=Ann(g), T=Ann(t), L=LCLM(G,T).
The primitive coefficient tuple of L has order 5, maximum degree 22, and
no common polynomial factor. All coefficients and derivative rows are in
x29_exact.json. The exact identities L mod G=0 and L(f)=0 in this connection
are checked. The first five observed derivative rows have rank five.

**Minimality:** 1,l,t are independent over Q(u): l is logarithmic at -1;
t has a log(-u) singularity at 0 while l is analytic there, so t cannot be
a rational combination of 1,l. E is the irreducible rank-two Legendre
module after its algebraic normalization, hence it has no constituent in
the unipotent Tate module. Its nonzero observed g generates rank two.
Consequently this particular f has minimal order five. At that order the
primitive polynomial annihilator is unique up to a constant, so degree 22
is minimal. “Smallest (r,d)” here means order first, then degree; no claim
about all higher-order/lower-degree Pareto alternatives is needed.

## Reusable x29_ftest.sage

Load the script, construct an operator L in its OA=Q(u)<Du>, then call
`ftest(L)`. It factors L with ore_algebra and tests every rank-two
composition factor F against both

    E = 4u^2(u^2-1)D^2+4u(3u^2-1)D+4u^2+1,
    Et = 4(u^2-1)D^2+8uD+1.

This checks all composition positions, so it includes left/right factors
and LCLM summands, rather than only a right divisor of the original L.
For each candidate, solve

    H' = C_F H - H C_target

for rational 2x2 H, where C denotes the companion matrix. The four-entry
Hom connection is converted to a scalar equation by a cyclic observable;
ore_algebra rational_solutions computes its complete rational horizontal
space without imposed degree/denominator bounds. Each reconstructed
horizontal vector and each invertible gauge are checked by exact
substitution. An invertible H is a positive certificate. Local exponents
alone are never used as an equivalence certificate.

For G, one gauge returned is minus the obvious jet map whose first row is
(1+u,u). Automatic factorization of the FULL L instead returns factors of
orders (2,1,1,1); the first, LEFT factor is gauge-equivalent to E by the
larger exact matrix recorded in x29_controls.json. This demonstrates that
the detector is not relying on the construction's known right factor G.
Both products and gauge identities are checked exactly. Independent tests
place E in the middle and left, detect Et, reject pure Tate operators, and
return UNDECIDED for an insufficient supplied high-order factorization.
The rational Hom between E and Et is zero; the square-root twist is thus
explicitly tested as a distinct target.

**Scope of the decision:** over Q(u), a successful complete factorization
and rational-solution solve decide presence/absence. `factors=[...]` may
supply an exact product to save work. With supplied factors of order <=2,
a missing gauge already proves absence because the targets are simple;
with unresolved higher-order supplied factors the result is UNDECIDED.
Failures or inability to construct a cyclic observable raise exceptions,
which must likewise be treated as UNDECIDED. ore_algebra's factor routine
is seminumeric and does not promise termination for arbitrary non-Fuchsian
operators; algebraic constant extensions that cannot coerce to Q(u) also
require further handling. Thus this is an executable exact-certificate
route for the intended rational Fuchsian inputs, not an unconditional
terminating algorithm for every conceivable exact operator. It recognizes
rational de Rham gauges induced by isogenies over the base field and the
specified twist, not arbitrary algebraic pullbacks or all twists.

## Optional kernel question: a precise literal equality is refuted

Specify the direct candidate rather than leaving “rotation average”
ambiguous. In separated coordinates put

    x(X,Y)=(cos((X+Y)/2), cos((Y-X)/2)),  b=3pi/2,
    A=x(b,Y).x(X,eta),
    D=sqrt(1-|x(b,Y)|^2)*sqrt(1-|x(X,eta)|^2).

The raw angular integral of Delta G is

    F(Y;X,eta)=int_0^(2pi) T3(A+D cos(phi))
                            /(2pi sqrt(1-(A+D cos(phi))^2)) dphi.

Since |x(b,Y)|^2=1, D=0, and its direct analytic limit off branch points is
F=T3(A)/sqrt(1-A^2). At Y=b the target is (0,1), so
A=cos((eta-X)/2). Choose X=pi, eta=5pi/6, both inside I=(pi/2,3pi/2).
Then, on the positive root branch,

    F(b;pi,5pi/6)=cos(pi/4)/sin(pi/12)=sqrt(3)+1 != 0.

The exact radical identity is checked in x29_controls.sage. A mean instead
of an angular integral only inserts 1/(2pi), and Delta T only inserts -1/4;
neither removes this nonzero value. The alternative root branch changes
its sign. There is a neighborhood of this off-diagonal point with no
square-root pinching and nonzero smooth limit.

In contrast, the defining regular-endpoint spectral product satisfies
K32(b;X,eta)=delta(X-eta) in the source measure d eta. Its restriction to
that neighborhood is zero as a distribution. Testing against a smooth
function supported there proves the contradiction; no convergence of raw
pointwise spectral truncations is invoked. Therefore **PROVED:** this
literal direct rotation average cannot equal K32, even up to a nonzero
constant. This does NOT exclude a formula taking an additional boundary
jump/logarithmic residue, changing the relative cycle, applying a
projection, or adding a characteristic-support prescription. Those are
additional operations, not supplied by the raw rotation average. Their
possible construction and the evaluated K32 remain **OPEN**.
