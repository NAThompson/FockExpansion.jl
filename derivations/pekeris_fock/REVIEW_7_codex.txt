1. Atoms, parity, evaluator and independent spot checks — CO-SIGN, numerical.
Lt implements Ltilde=L+(pi-2)/pi*log(R/s). alp=alpha-pi/2 and U are
exchange odd; V and Lt are even. build3 uses odd x powers for alp/U and even
powers for Lt/V, so the products are singlets. Folding to x>=0 and doubling
is correct for the total weak integrands, including the derivative cross
terms. Independent signed-distance evaluations verify all four parities at
two interior points (review7_parity.out). These are checks of the actual
letter functions, not a global branch/domain theorem.

run3.jl sets precision before include (384 bits by default), sets the psi30
rtol to 1e-50, and uses the BigFloat/ForwardDiff path. The Float64 helper and
the disabled zero-gradient block are not used by this driver. check3.out
reports 30-node value agreement 1.71e-57 between precisions, x-gradient
finite-difference discrepancy 7.77e-47, and Float64 discrepancy 1.90e-15.
These interior checks support evaluator consistency, not uniform endpoint
error bounds. The quadrature excludes very small endpoint strips, so saved
quadrature refinement remains relevant. min5_h24 repeats the printed min5
energies, with 448 bits reported in COLLAB; the .out itself does not encode
precision. An inverse-iteration residual alone does not certify a rigorous
lower/upper enclosure or prove that the computed root is the lowest root.

2. Old sub-E0 energies — CO-SIGN mechanism, qualify causal isolation.
The variational upper bound holds for every nonzero form-domain trial
function, regardless of the E/a21 parameters used to define it. Parameter
inputs cannot themselves defeat that theorem. BigFloat reevaluation removes
the reported negative errors: atoms2psi N8/9/10 now has positive errors
6.8279e-16, 1.0588e-17, 2.1588e-19. This strongly supports numerical
contamination in the old Float64 implementation, consistent with finite
value errors, inconsistent/clamped/zeroed gradients and severe conditioning.
It does not separately identify the contribution of each defect, nor does
positivity alone prove an exact variational enclosure. AD differentiation
of a consistent approximate atom would still define a legitimate trial
function; the problem is inconsistent form evaluation/numerical error, not
merely the use of an approximate function.

3. E0/a21 independence — CO-SIGN EXACTLY for these finite trial spans.
The repository psi30_parts has all E,a21 dependence explicitly in `state`;
its classical and elliptic terms are independent of both. With Q=r1^2+r2^2,
S=r1+r2,c=r12, homogenization gives
 d(R^3 psi30)/dE = Z*S*(2Q+r1*r2)/18-c*(6Q-c^2)/72,
 d(R^3 psi30)/da21 = -Z*S*(Q-c^2)/2+c*(6Q-5c^2)/12.
Both are exchange-even homogeneous cubics. Fresh exact SymPy checks are in
review7_algebra.py/.out. The Float64 least-squares residuals in param_check
are supporting evidence, not the proof.
For every enriched term s^n*x^a*y^b*psi30h, build3 has n>=3 and a+b<=n-3.
Multiplying either cubic by s^(n-3)*x^a*y^b yields only plain-space terms
s^n*x^j*y^k, j even, j+k<=n. Those functions already belong to the included
polynomial space (n<=N-2<=N). Changing E/a21 therefore preserves the exact
finite span for both atoms2psi and min5psi; this is stronger than saying
that parameter inputs do not violate the upper bound. The Ritz minimizer
and its a21 functional are independent of those atom parameters in exact
arithmetic. Numerical conditioning may still change computed answers.
The supplied reference E0 is used to display energy errors, not predicted
independently to all its digits.

5. Codex's phase-3 status — unchanged and incomplete.
psi30 has an explicit finite local algebraic connection; restriction of
scalars and denominator clearing give finite local polynomial differential
and Laguerre stencils on nonsingular charts. The 216-generator combined
algebraic-field bound is not a minimal rank. Minimal rank, derivative-row
stabilization, global endpoint/electron-fold interfaces, actual global atom
basis, and expanded global stencil counts/bandwidth remain open.
psi41^(2) has a finite holonomic extension route via its elementary 1D
Feynman integral, with endpoint and harmonic normalization retained;
membership in the psi30 module is unproved. psi40 has a further Green/direct-
image route, not an explicit reduced connection; its a40/a42 harmonic data
must be specified. Neither fixed-order route proves a finite all-order
alphabet, tower convergence, form-core completeness, growing-block
Pincherle selection, or a21 continuity from energy error.
