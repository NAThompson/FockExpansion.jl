Claims to check:
1. cuspexterior is overlapping Taylor panels (radius limited ~R by the singular
   point at 0) plus an approximate 2-term BC at rmax: an ODE integrator in
   series form, not a single global representation.
2. Fock/origin data cannot reach large R regardless of resummation: at fixed E
   the regular solution mixes exp(-kappa R) and exp(+kappa R) components, so the
   map (E, free amplitudes) -> Phi(R) amplifies data errors by ~exp(2 kappa R)
   (~2 digits per unit R for kappa~2.4). Rounding cancellation is the fixable
   part; data sensitivity is not. Hence interior + exterior + matching is the
   right architecture.
3. Proposal: in the cusp gauge, Phi''+(5/R+2U)Phi'+(-Lambda/R^2+D/R+T)Phi=0 has a
   regular singular point at 0 and a rank-1 irregular point at infinity. Each
   decaying solution exp(lambda_i R) R^{-mu_i} sum h_j R^{-j} (lambda_i the
   decaying roots of det(lambda^2+2U lambda+T)=0) has a Borel transform with
   finite radius = distance to the nearest other root. Re-expanding as an
   inverse factorial (Norlund/Watson) series gives a convergent exterior series
   on [R0,inf), R0>0, with algebraically generated coefficients, replacing
   panels and the finite-rmax BC. Fock (converges for all R) + factorial series,
   matched on one sphere, gives a two-series closed form within the channel model.
   Equivalently: the Laplace transform in R gives a Fuchsian system in s whose
   singular points are the 2m roots.
4. Caveat: in the projected model every channel decays like exp(-sqrt(-2E)R)
   (~2.41) [or the gauge-shifted analog], but true helium's outer electron
   decays like exp(-sqrt(2(E+2)) r2) (~1.34); fixed hyperspherical channels need
   ever more channels as R grows. A truly global exterior must build in the He+
   threshold structure.
